**Coverage only; no chart-scored serving rows.** Research registry tracks separate
accepted, archived ordinary SDK source-present **150+8 v1** and **150+64 v2**
initial screens, not confirmation. Each completed six fresh clients: three per parallel uniform-residue
control and parallel row-residue candidate. Five owners remain explicit: client,
inference, preparation, boundary A and boundary B. Neither screen borrows samples,
identity, RAM qualification or status from earlier decode/byte-dot cohorts.

## Admission gate for a future read-only adapter

Preserve and hash-bind original raw report, frozen plan, terminal receipt and
summary separately. Summary hashes alone do not establish evidence validity.
Mirror `scripts/summarize_private_boundary_row_codec.py` and its exact verifier
closure in `benchmarks/research/private_boundary_row_codec.py`, including:

- Completed six-cell balanced order (control/0, candidate/0, candidate/1,
  control/1, candidate/2, control/2), same private cohort and frozen suite/build.
- Terminal receipt canonical bindings to raw report, plan and public prompt;
  completed post-run source checks; exact frozen verifier inventory and SDK
  source hashes bound to recorded build. A receipt is not a signature.
- Exact two-Experiment allowlist: only row encoding and candidate certificate
  pin differ. Authenticate archived certificate **actual bytes**, digest and
  size; bounded envelope, metadata and width decompression; exact source/numeric
  layout. Check independently approved native pipeline/composition/schedule/
  descriptor/body commitments, never commitments copied from audited worker.
- Per-cell workload binding and measurement version reconstruction, common
  opaque private-input commitment, public fixture declaration/counts. Public
  prompt SHA256 is not proof of private salted prompt preimage.
- Exact full logits, active KV/history, RNG, selections and outputs across
  codecs while allowing their different numeric-body fingerprints. Natural EOS
  selections are not emitted outputs; actual output count and cap stay separate.
- Raw owner CPU, directed native TCP, application-body conservation, head
  payload/header/envelope and paired residual reconciliation. Keep unallocated
  residuals; never assume zero. Verify all cached samples/medians/comparisons.
- Largest finalized complete-client lifetime RSS across three samples, not
  median or child-only RSS, for observed tiers; zero observed swap growth.

Existing byte-dot reader assumes common numeric bodies and unchanged head reply
bytes. Relabeling row-codec reports to pass that reader would remove essential
checks. Current router therefore rejects row-codec scoring explicitly; typed
coverage stays outside ranked `cohorts` until an independent adapter implements
these gates. Existing cohorts remain unchanged.

## Claim limits

Certificate publication/delivery locations and missing owners are declared setup
scenarios. Fixed-byte budget is actual certificate size times declared missing
owners, **not measured publisher traffic**. Publisher distribution bytes/time remain
unknown. Derivation CPU is null in response records; the benchmark reference
retains separate offline publication measurements. Source certification is a trusted publisher assertion,
not a cryptographic quantization proof. No cold-distribution completeness,
physical-wire, Internet RTT, independent-operator, representative-quality or
global SOTA claim follows from these initial source-present screens.
