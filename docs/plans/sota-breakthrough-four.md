# Four frontier-breaking research gates

## Objective

Explore mechanisms that can improve several serving objectives together under
the existing Low (256 MiB) and Medium (2 GiB) whole-client memory envelopes.
Fresh-request delivery, first-text latency, decode throughput, client CPU/RSS
and Preparation CPU/RSS retain separate accounting. Existing measured
correlations are observations about tested configurations, not constraints on
future implementations.

The user requested four Sol 6.1 investigations. Their outputs must advance the
existing gates with executable bounded checks or a concrete construction
decision, rather than repeat earlier proposals.

## Training requirement

Trainable artifacts may be specialized per supported public model/checkpoint.
A universal learned model is not required. Shared training and evaluation
infrastructure remains model-neutral, with source-specific dimensions,
tokenizers and capabilities discovered from locked metadata.

Every proposed tiny model has **strictly fewer than 1,000,000,000 total runtime
parameters**, including frozen backbones, embeddings and output components.
Trainable-adapter counts do not substitute for complete model size. Count all
additional client artifacts and allocations, including quantization buffers,
tokenizer, state and fallback. A helper and a standalone replacement have
different execution/quality contracts and must be identified explicitly.

Support mapping distinguishes complete runtime/checkpoint coverage from semantic
lowering alone. Training begins with a resource-admitted representative pilot;
other checkpoints retain explicit untrained or blocked status. Public training
and disjoint held-out evaluation must test useful language behavior as well as
copying, rare tokens, order and causal generation. A synthetic fixture is only a
mechanics check. Changed models or compressed inputs do not inherit exact source
logits/KV, arbitrary sampling, generation quality or serving performance.

Before fitting, each pilot records its primary task metric and numerical
acceptance margin. Compare full original, zero-training compressed original and
learned-compressed original inputs; if target adaptation is introduced, add both
full adapted and compressed adapted controls. This separates adaptation damage
from compression damage. Quality margins cannot be selected after held-out results.

## Execution and evidence

Small synthetic correctness checks may run independently. Real checkpoint value
loading, sustained training, timing cohorts, native builds and complete responses
run serially under parent control, existing host reserve/swap admission and the
shared frontier measurement lock. Public metadata inspection is separate from
tensor loading. Original attempts and evidence stay intact; use fresh paths.

Software-private consumer execution remains the target. Private activations or
latents cannot be exposed to a provider. Fresh masks, role separation, exact
bindings, replay rejection and failure retirement retain their original contracts.
Upstream papers/code are specifications and oracles, not vendored runtime methods.

For unchanged execution, preserve complete applicable integer/float32 outputs,
logits, KV and sampler state. For a changed contract, define its independent
oracle and quality gate. Charge missing public artifacts, setup, one-use material,
all directed traffic, extra owners, discarded capacity and fallback. Analytical
floors, synthetic checks, operator/transport fixtures and complete SDK cohorts
remain separately labeled. A bounded screen alone establishes no new serving
SOTA or client-memory qualification.

Each measured arm freezes source/numeric identity, workload, exact build,
topology, lifecycle and per-party cache inventory. State which checkpoint,
tokenizer and derived artifacts are pre-positioned. Source-present historical
controls do not supply unmeasured source-cold costs. Counter/event windows and
fresh-process lifetime peaks stay explicit; Low/Medium eligibility uses the
largest complete-client peak across every replicate, never a median.

For a serving comparison, report the existing seven objective directions and
complete-request latency/aggregate CPU. A new nondominated point can extend the
frontier without dominating its control. Claim domination only when every
comparable objective is no worse and at least one is better under the frozen
comparison rule; missing costs cannot establish domination. A changed topology
retains its separate trust contract and leaders. Independently confirm any
winning exploratory serving result before promoting its evidence tier.
