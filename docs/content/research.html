Research for high-performance private LLM inference.

PLLM is a high-performance private LLM multi-party inference runtime and extensible experimentation system. Typed component and provider contracts, immutable experiments, search, and benchmarking turn credible research into reproducible results.

A runtime and a research system.

The runtime keeps plaintext, model state, masks, and decoding inside the client boundary while separate services perform prepared masked computation. The research system turns pinned methods into reusable extensions, immutable plans, assurance records, and matched benchmark evidence.

Extend by capability

Methods enter through stable component and provider contracts, not paper-specific runtime branches. Implementations remain independent of upstream research code.

Search valid experiments

Immutable configurations support bounded grid and seeded random search without bypassing compatibility, correctness, privacy, or quality gates.

Compare matched evidence

Benchmarks bind results to exact plans and cohorts so eligible state-of-the-art baselines can be compared under equivalent conditions.
