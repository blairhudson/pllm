---
title: "Reading research and evidence"
description: "Learn how PLLM connects papers, clean-room implementations, measurements, and publication claims."
---

PLLM records each step from a paper to a published result. It keeps the original
source, clean-room method, component version, plan history, benchmark evidence,
assurance evidence, and proposed claim separate. Citing a paper does not mean it
was reproduced. Passing a unit test does not mean a complete model is supported.
A benchmark does not prove privacy.

Compare results only when the model, model-body fingerprint, plan, numeric policy,
workload, environment, cold or warm mode, and metric definitions match. Keep
limitations and failed attempts in the record. Missing evidence is not a pass.
