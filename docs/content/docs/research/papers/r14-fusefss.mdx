## Source contribution

FuseFSS targets fusion and efficiency of secure nonlinear/helper operations in FSS-based LLM inference.

## PLLM perspective

Acquire and pin the full source and artifact; port complete fused helpers with their offline key generation, remaining secure multiplication and rescaling.

Prior reference fused-polynomial experiments are alternative methods, not a FuseFSS implementation.

This paper remains tracked. Named modules below are implementation targets, not finished PLLM components; no reproduction, integration, assurance, or matched benchmark is claimed.

The source's boundary remains binding: A fused key lookup does not make subsequent private polynomial evaluation free. Public model support does not eliminate the second online role.
