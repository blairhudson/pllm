---
title: "Evidence and records"
description: "Keep sources, methods, implementations, plans, evidence, assurance, and claims independently reviewable."
---

PLLM research uses immutable, linked records rather than one undifferentiated
success status. A paper can be acquired without being reproduced. A component can
be implemented without benchmark parity, protected execution, or assurance. A
measurement can be valid without supporting a broad publication claim.

Use this section to inspect the record types, evidence rules, comparison metrics,
and generated catalog. Every public claim should resolve to the exact source,
implementation, plan, workload, environment, result, and limitation that support
it.
