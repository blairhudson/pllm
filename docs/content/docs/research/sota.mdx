## Latest combination screen

Thirty fresh Qwen2.5 clients completed all sixty cold/repeat responses at 150/288
input tokens plus eight outputs. Compared with the matched maintained control:

| Combination | Client lifetime RAM | Preparation cold CPU | Cold online TTFT | Cold + repeat TCP traffic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

the lowest observed client RAM. Outputs match and observed swap growth is zero.

## Reading the table

Comparisons use one checked cohort at a time: matching source, numeric contract,
workload, output identity and measurement lifecycle. Separate invocations, changed
numerics and different topologies keep their own leaders. Sample counts and
measurement windows remain explicit; these observations are not confidence intervals or an exhaustive search
of every possible combination. Quality and privacy depend on the linked contracts.

Base and output-dependent traffic require an attributed ledger, including prepaid
and unused output material. Complete headline rates stay **Unattributed** when
evidence is missing or partial. The new screen's expandable **application-body
traffic** details show known base, known output-dependent, residual and total bytes
for each replicate. TCP totals and whole-response averages cannot fill those gaps.
Available public artifact sizes appear beside the combination; their distribution
remains outside response counters.
