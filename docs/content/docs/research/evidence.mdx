---
title: "Research evidence"
description: "Understand which evidence supports fidelity, performance, privacy, and deployment claims."
---

PLLM records source claims, clean-room fidelity, native implementation agreement,
compiler coverage, runtime support, generation quality,
[matched benchmarks](/sdk/evaluate/evidence/),
[assurance](/sdk/evaluate/assurance/), and deployment observations separately.
Missing evidence is unknown; it is not a zero result.

Every comparison should name its [research objective](/research/problems/) and
retain the costs that move in the opposite direction. Measure client and
Preparation CPU/RSS, first-token delay, decode throughput, and base plus
output-dependent all-link traffic under stated lifecycle boundaries. When the
traffic split is unavailable, preserve the measured total and mark attribution
unknown. A whole-response average or an online-only decode counter is a different
quantity.

Accepted records live under [`docs/evidence/`](https://github.com/blairhudson/pllm/tree/main/docs/evidence).
Each record keeps its environment, workload, software revision, and limitations. Canonical full benchmark records use `pllm.benchmark_result.v1`; `EvidenceRegistry` retains positive, negative, and explicitly unavailable results and queries only exact recorded cohorts.
A result does not apply to a changed component, plan, model, parameter, or
deployment unless a new validation explicitly connects them.

Keep negative and unavailable results in the record. `not_refuted` means only that
the named attack failed within the recorded budget.
