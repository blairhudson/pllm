## Checked decoder configurations

- [Dense Qwen3 (Qwen3-0.6B)](/sdk/models/families/qwen3/): part of its bounded complete baseline schedule. Pinned Qwen3-0.6B checkpoint imported and executed through 114 compiled baseline stages, prefill and decode; tiny compiled two-role protocol test and scoped clear-reference probes, no real-checkpoint provider benchmark **Next:** W4A4 fails top-token parity on two checked prompts; the local-role default W8A8 also fails one short prompt. Quantization-quality, real-checkpoint serving and matched benchmark evidence, and protected whole-decoder execution remain open.
- [Qwen3.5-4B text decoder](/sdk/models/families/qwen3-5/): part of its bounded complete baseline schedule. Pinned official Qwen3.5-4B text checkpoint binds 426 required BF16/F32 artifacts to 130 compiled stages. Two real-checkpoint prefill/decode prefixes score 2/4 same-token W8A8 top-1 against upstream BF16 (3.66 worst absolute logit error); a float32-body diagnostic with W8 token/head boundary scores 3/4 (0.46 worst error). A real-checkpoint prepared SDK and gateway request each completes through two local role children with masked stage traffic and zero plaintext prompt/token-ID bytes sent. A generated tiny checkpoint independently matches the upstream float32 Torch reference below 0.05 W8A8 worst logit error. Text-only partial MRoPE matches checked Torch positions **Next:** Four same-token selections do not establish representative generation quality; W8A8 still misses two. Full-wire and full-response compute costs, protected recurrence, independent providers and independent image/video position axes remain unchecked.
- [Gemma 4 E2B text decoder](/sdk/models/families/gemma-4/): part of its bounded complete baseline schedule. Pinned E2B: 213 checkpoint-bound compiled stages, two local-clear W8A8 prefill-to-decode runs; 2/2 same-token top-1, 0.70 mean top-5 recall and 3.5625 worst logit error against BF16; one prepared HTTP request completes through separate in-process inference and preparation services with masked online traffic **Next:** Two-child deployment, independent-provider privacy, representative generation quality and matched cost evidence.
- [Gemma 4 E4B text decoder](/sdk/models/families/gemma-4/): part of its bounded complete baseline schedule. Pinned complete compiler prefill/decode schedule and isolated BF16 tests; E2B checkpoint executes locally but E4B has no checkpoint execution **Next:** E4B checkpoint import, provider-backed prepared execution, quality and cost evidence.
