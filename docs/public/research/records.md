# Evidence and records

Keep sources, methods, implementations, plans, evidence, assurance, and claims independently reviewable.

[View canonical HTML](https://pllm.run/research/records/)

Document ID: `pllm.docs.research.records`  
Release: `0.1.0`

PLLM research uses immutable, linked records rather than one undifferentiated
success status. A paper can be acquired without being reproduced. A component can
be implemented without benchmark parity, protected execution, or assurance. A
measurement can be valid without supporting a broad publication claim.

Use this section to inspect evidence rules and comparison metrics. Source status
lives in the [paper catalog](/research/papers/) and [research backlog](/research/backlog/).
Every public claim should resolve to the exact source, implementation, plan,
workload, environment, result, and limitation that support it.
