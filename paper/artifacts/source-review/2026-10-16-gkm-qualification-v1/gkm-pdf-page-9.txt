4.3     Handling the Embedding Layer

LLMs also use an embedding layer, which converts an input token w into its cor-
responding embedding T [w] using a lookup in a (public) table T . For vocabulary
size V and embedding dimension d, the table T consists of V entries in Rd . For,
e.g., Llama 70B, V = 128000 and d = 8192 which results in a table of size 2GB.
In principle, then, the embedding operation could be done locally by the client
as long as they have 2GB of memory. Nevertheless, we provide a private, veri-
fiable outsourcing protocol for the embedding layer to support devices that do
not have this much memory, or to support future models with larger tables.
