3.4     One-Hot Garbling
One-Hot Garbling [13] is a recent GC improvement that goes beyond Boolean gate evaluation. The technique
allows privacy-free (i.e. with E learning the accessed index) GC LUTs where communication is logarithmic
in the number of LUT rows N . The technique reduces communication consumption via so-called garbled
one-hot encodings. We build on top of one-hot garbling to achieve efficient privacy-preserving GC LUT.
